Where should the tilt servo live?

Front views, drawn roughly to scale (1 grid = 10 mm). Both share the same head, neck and base. The difference is the tilt servo.

A: Servo in the arm

The tilt servo hides in the left arm and drives the head's side. The right arm carries a pin bearing. The head only contains electronics.

+ Lightest head, best balance. The servo cable never moves with the head. The head is easy to open.
− Wider, about 82 mm. Chunky "shoulders".

B: Servo inside the head

The tilt servo sits inside the head behind the cam board and turns the head against thin, slim arms.

+ Narrower, about 64 mm. Cleaner Wall-E silhouette.
− The head gets deeper and heavier. The servo cable flexes with every tilt. Crowded with the Dupont plugs. Harder to assemble.

Side view: tilt range (same for both)

The pivot sits near the head's center of mass so a small servo holds it steady. The camera looks out the front, and the USB-C opening is on the head's side.
